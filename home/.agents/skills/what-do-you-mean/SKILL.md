---
name: what-do-you-mean
description:
  Clarify the agent's preceding statement or question with enough context for
  the user to understand and respond. Use when the user asks what something
  means, why it matters, or requests more context about an unresolved point.
---

Clarify the unresolved point the user is referring to so they understand its
meaning, purpose, and consequences and can continue the existing conversation.

**Focus.** Identify the statement, question, or decision from the latest
exchange or the user's explicit reference.

**Explanation.** Explain why the point matters, define its relevant terms, and
make its implications and tradeoffs concrete enough for the user to reason
about it.

**Continuation.** Restate the unresolved question or decision with enough
context to answer it, then resume the existing workflow and conversation mode.
